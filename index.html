About

Beyond the code.

I studied Computer Science at Federal University Lokoja and I’m a technology enthusiast who enjoys building things that solve real-world problems. My work sits at the intersection of software development, machine learning, and artificial intelligence, with a growing interest in how intelligent systems can be made more useful, efficient, and accessible

I enjoy the process behind building technology just as much as the final product. I like taking an idea, understanding the problem beneath it, experimenting with different approaches, and turning what works into something people can actually use. Along the way, I have worked on projects involving computer vision, predictive modelling, web applications, and AI, giving me the opportunity to learn through both successful experiments and things that did not work the first time.

Beyond writing code, I value teaching, research, leadership, and collaboration. I have taught Computer Science, led community projects, and worked with people from different backgrounds to turn ideas into practical outcomes. These experiences have shaped how I approach technology: with curiosity, responsibility, and a focus on creating value.

I’m building my career as a Software and Machine Learning Engineer, combining software development with machine learning and artificial intelligence to build practical, intelligent solutions. I’m interested in creating systems that are not only well built, but can also learn from data and solve meaningful real-world problems. My goal is simple: keep learning, keep building, and use technology to create solutions that have a meaningful real-world impact.

Research

Research & Publications

Software Engineering

Human-Computer Interaction
